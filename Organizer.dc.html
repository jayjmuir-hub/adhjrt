Leave a date empty to leave it undecided. With no opening date, registration stays closed — which is what you want until the date is agreed. The date under each box is what the public page will print, spelled out, so a mis-typed month is visible straight away.

Which one is in charge

Simulate a tournament
For testing only. Publishes every age group, records a walkover (the first team listed wins) for every pool match, generates the knockout stage from the resulting standings and walks that over too — finals included — then publishes again so the finished bracket is visible everywhere.
This overwrites any score already recorded for a match, real or otherwise. Reset the simulation undoes it — unpublishes every group, removes every result and clears the generated bracket — but leaves saved pools and team assignments untouched.

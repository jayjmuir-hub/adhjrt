## What is on the site that will bite you

**Three age-group pickers, one design, no shared code:**

| | |
|---|---|
| `/app` | `pills()` in `app.html` |
| `/scores` | `ageDayBlocks` in `Scores & Standings.dc.html` |
| homepage Fixtures | `fixtureAgeDayBlocks` in `Quins JRT.dc.html` |

- ⚠️ **THE DAY SPLIT IS DERIVED FROM THE VENUE LAYOUT, NEVER TYPED.** A group is
  on Saturday **because that is where it holds pitches**. `app.html` once
  carried `const SATURDAY = [...]` and had U12G and both U18 groups on the wrong
  day, on the public site, with registration open.
- ⚠️ **The homepage keeps all FIFTEEN groups; `/scores` keeps THIRTEEN.** Not an
  inconsistency to tidy — U6/U7 have fixtures but no standings. Asserted both ways.
- ⚠️ **The open day follows the selection**, and the pin is stored **with the
  selection it was made under** so a new pick releases it.
- ⚠️ **A closed day that holds the pick names it.** Otherwise the picker lies
  about where you are.

**`/manager`'s `loadDraw()` opens with an ENTRY GUARD and it is load-bearing.**
It looks like a duplicate of the stale-response guard after the fetch and is
not — **a guard placed after the await is not a guard against what the await let
happen.** Only an organiser gets the age switcher and it is never disabled.

**The About section is the most heavily-trapped part of the homepage** — the
photo box bleeds past its grid column and subtracts `--sbw`; the crest is the
HOLED `crest-shield.png` and stands or falls with the bat; `isolation:isolate`
is what keeps the gradient border below the crest; the carousel is purely
automatic, so `prefers-reduced-motion` does NOT stop the timer.

⚠️ **The engine rewrites your JavaScript** — whitespace + camelCase + `=` becomes
`sc-camel-…` inside inline `<script>` bodies in a `.dc.html`. Use lowercase or
snake_case. (The `<script type="text/x-dc">` component block is exempt.)

⚠️ **The engine re-renders the body after first paint, more than once.** Any
boot scan must keep re-scanning; any flag must be set at the END.

## `/app` on a phone — two findings, neither a repo bug

- **The app rendered in its DESKTOP layout** because the browser was in
  **"Desktop site" mode** — a 980px viewport, solved by measuring the gutters in
  the screenshot (59/58px of 1440 → `900 / (1 − 2f)` = **979.6**). ⚠️ Chrome
  stores that setting **per origin and an installed PWA inherits it**.
- **Google Play Protect blocks the PWA install from Samsung Internet** —
  a `targetSdkVersion` complaint about the generated APK, **not** a verdict on
  adhjrt.com, and **nothing in this repo can fix it**. Install from Chrome.
  ⚠️ Good argument for continuing not to promote the install.

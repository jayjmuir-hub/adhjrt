What is already settled

  • Age-grade format under UAERF rules. Mini, midi and youth, from U6 through to U18.
  • Two days. Saturday 7 and Sunday 8 November 2026, at Zayed Sports City, Abu Dhabi.
  • A pool stage, so one defeat is not the end of your day. Every team plays every other team in its pool. Finals are played on top of the pool stage; which sides reach them, and how the brackets are named, depends on the age group and on how many teams enter — that is confirmed once entries close.
  • Spirit of Rugby. Sides nominate an opposition player in the older age groups; it is judged on the day.
Playing regulations come from the UAE Rugby Federation age-grade framework. Anything the tournament decides on top of that — squad sizes, match length, the play-up allowance — will be set out here rather than left to be asked about on the morning.
